Average human proportions can be expressed mathematically using total height
as the base unit.

The head from top to chin is one eighth of total height. The neck adds roughly
one thirty-second. The torso from shoulders to crotch takes up three eighths
of total height. The upper leg from crotch to knee is one quarter, and the
lower leg from knee to sole is another quarter. This means the legs account
for half of total height and the midpoint of the body falls at the crotch.

In the classical system the body is eight head lengths tall. The chin falls at
one head from the top. The nipple line sits at two heads down. The navel is
at three heads. The crotch is at four heads, which is the halfway point.
Mid-thigh is at five heads. Just below the knee is six heads. Mid-shin is
seven heads and the ground is eight.

For widths, shoulder width is roughly three eighths of total height or about
three head widths across. Hip width is about one quarter of total height or
two head widths. The head itself is about three quarters as wide as it is
tall. Waist width falls around five sixteenths of height.

Arm span is approximately equal to total height, which is the famous Vitruvian
ratio. The upper arm from shoulder to elbow is about three sixteenths of
height. The forearm from elbow to wrist is about one eighth of height. Hand
length is roughly one tenth of height, which is close to the length of the
face. Foot length is about one seventh of total height.

There are several relationships that approximate the golden ratio of 1.618 to

1. The distance from navel to floor compared to the distance from the top of
   the head to the navel is roughly this ratio. The same applies to shoulder to
   fingertip compared to elbow to fingertip, and hip to floor compared to knee to
   floor.

Some simple summary ratios are arm span to height equals one to one, legs to
torso plus head equals one to one, upper leg to lower leg equals one to one,
upper arm to forearm plus hand equals one to one, shoulder width to head width
equals three to one, head to total height equals one to eight, foot to total
height equals one to seven, and hand to total height equals one to ten.
